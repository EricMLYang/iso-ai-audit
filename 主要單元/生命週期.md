## 2. 箭頭流向與邏輯關係



* **主要循序流程**：`Inception 起始` → `Design (設計) and Development (開發)` → `Verification (查證) and Validation (確認)` → `Deployment 部署` → `Operation (營運) and Monitoring (監督)` → `Retirement 汰除`

* **雙向流程**：`Operation (營運) and Monitoring (監督)` ↔ `Continuous validation 持續確認`

* **反饋迴路 (Feedback Loop)**：`Operation (營運) and Monitoring (監督)` → `Re-evaluation 重新評估`，並由此回流指向 `Inception 起始` 與 `Design (設計) and Development (開發)`
